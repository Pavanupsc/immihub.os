Getting Started

The basics of ImmiHub and how it works.

What is ImmiHub?+

ImmiHub is a secure mobile app that helps H-1B visa holders and their families manage critical immigration documents and never miss an expiry date. Upload your documents, we extract key dates with OCR, and you get smart reminders weeks and months before deadlines.

How is ImmiHub different from a regular cloud storage app?+

Generic cloud storage just holds your files. ImmiHub understands them. We automatically pull expiry dates from passports, I-797s, I-94s, EADs, and visa stamps, then build a personalized compliance timeline with proactive reminders. It's purpose-built for the U.S. immigration journey.

How do I sign up?+

We're currently in private beta. Join the waitlist on this page and we'll send you a download link as soon as your invitation opens up.

Security & Privacy

How we protect your most sensitive documents.

How are my documents stored and protected?+

Your documents are encrypted at rest in AWS S3 and your profile data lives in AWS RDS (PostgreSQL), both hosted in the United States. All traffic is encrypted in transit with TLS. Authentication is phone-number-based with one-time passcode verification.

Will ImmiHub share my information?+

No. Your documents and personal data are never shared with third parties without your explicit consent. We're not affiliated with USCIS or any government agency, and we never will be.

What happens during OCR processing?+

OCR extraction is performed by AWS Textract. We pull only the dates and document metadata we need to power reminders. Raw OCR output is not retained beyond the extraction step. You always confirm the extracted dates before reminders are activated.

Can I delete my data?+

Yes — anytime. You can delete individual documents, your full document set, or your entire account from inside the app. You can also email support@getimmihub.com.

Features & Use

What ImmiHub can do for you today.

What documents can I upload?+

Passports, I-797 Approval Notices, I-94 Travel Records, U.S. Visa Stamps, and EAD cards. We accept clear photos and PDF scans.

How accurate is the date extraction?+

Our OCR is highly accurate on clear scans, but you always review and confirm extracted dates before reminders go live. Think of it as a smart first draft you sign off on.

How do reminders work?+

You'll get push notifications and email alerts at smart intervals — typically 90, 60, 30, 14, and 7 days before each expiry. Push delivery is handled by Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Can I manage documents for my family?+

Today, each user manages their own vault. Family vault sharing is on our near-term roadmap so spouses and dependents can stay coordinated in one place.

Eligibility

Who ImmiHub is built for today.

Is ImmiHub only for H-1B holders?+

Our launch focus is H-1B visa holders and their H-4 dependents — that's where the deadline pressure is most acute. F-1/OPT, L-1, O-1, and Green Card pathways are next on the roadmap.

Do I need to be in the U.S. to use ImmiHub?+

You can sign up from anywhere, but the app is purpose-built for navigating the U.S. immigration system.

Pricing & Access

What it costs and what's included.

Is ImmiHub free?+

Yes — core features are free for individual immigrants and always will be. We believe protecting your status shouldn't depend on your ability to pay.

Will there ever be paid plans?+

We may offer paid features for employers, universities, and immigration attorneys to help fund the platform — but the individual core experience stays free.
